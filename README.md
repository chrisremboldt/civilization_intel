# civilization_intel

Public research base for **financial, technological, and industrial dynamics** — how capital, tools, and production systems interact, and what those interactions imply for states, firms, and infrastructure.

This repository is a working archive: briefs, source notes, models, and follow-on questions. It is meant to accumulate, not to ship a product.

## Scope

- **Finance** — capital formation, credit, markets, fiscal and monetary constraints, ownership, and allocation.
- **Technology** — research, invention, diffusion, compute, energy, and the tools that change production possibility.
- **Industry** — capacity, supply chains, materials, labor, logistics, and the physical plant that turns inputs into output.

Work here should stay concrete: named actors, measurable quantities, dated sources, and explicit uncertainty.

## Working method

Each assignment should leave behind:

1. A short brief (question, claim, and why it matters)
2. Evidence with citations and dates
3. Mechanisms (what causes what, and under what constraints)
4. Open questions and what would change the conclusion
